AIRIV Indonesia Retail POS Rail

Indonesia POS Retail & F&B Engine

Indonesian Point of Sale layer for Odoo 18 with QRIS payment rails, 58/80mm thermal receipt configuration, PPN 12% receipt breakdown, NPWP/NIK store metadata, and WhatsApp digital receipt dispatch.

Odoo 18 CommunityPoint of SaleQRISThermal ReceiptAIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV POS Indonesia extends Odoo Point of Sale with Indonesian checkout controls, payment rail classification, statutory receipt metadata, and customer-facing WhatsApp receipt evidence.

Payment Rails

QRIS and Indonesian methods

Classify POS payment methods as cash IDR, dynamic QRIS, static QRIS, bank transfer, or EDC card rails with QRIS merchant metadata.

Receipt Control

58/80mm thermal and tax ready

Configure POS receipt paper width, store NPWP/NIK, and PPN 12% breakdown behavior for Indonesian retail and F&B counters.

Digital Receipt

WhatsApp customer proof

Send itemized POS receipt messages from paid orders through AIRIV WhatsApp integration when available, with fallback status tracking.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Configure POS

Set thermal paper width, store NPWP/NIK, statutory tax breakdown, and WhatsApp receipt preference on POS configuration.

2

Prepare payment rails

Create or review Indonesian payment methods such as QRIS Dynamic, Static QRIS, bank transfer, EDC, and cash IDR.

3

Close checkout

Run retail or F&B sales through Odoo POS while preserving customer, cashier, payment, and tax evidence.

4

Send receipt

Dispatch the official itemized WhatsApp receipt from paid POS orders and track sent status inside Odoo.

Installation Guidance

Install

Add to Odoo addons

Clone branch 18.0, add the module folder to your addons path, restart Odoo, update the apps list, and install the module.

Configure

Prepare POS settings

Set receipt paper width, store NPWP/NIK, tax breakdown option, and Indonesian payment methods.

Operate

Run checkout evidence

Process POS orders, validate payment rail selection, then send WhatsApp receipts for paid or done orders.

Configuration Checklist

POS dependencyConfirm Odoo Point of Sale and Accounting are installed.
Payment methodsReview QRIS Dynamic, transfer, EDC, and cash payment methods before go-live.
QRIS metadataFill QRIS merchant ID or static QRIS payload where applicable.
Receipt settingsChoose 58mm or 80mm thermal layout and fill store NPWP/NIK metadata.
WhatsApp receiptAssign customers with mobile or WhatsApp numbers before using digital receipt dispatch.
